# Prayers, Psalms & Songs for Every Situation 🙏📖🎵

**Tap what you're facing — and find the psalm to read, the song to sing, and the prayer to pray.** Every prayer, psalm and song of the King James Bible, gathered so you always know where to go.

> *"Is any among you afflicted? let him pray. Is any merry? let him sing psalms."* — James 5:13 (KJV)

## What's inside
- **🧭 Situations** — 30 real-life moments (afraid, guilty, grieving, under attack, thankful, waiting, sick, in love, can't sleep, celebrating a victory…). Tap one → the exact **psalms to read** and **songs to sing**, with each key verse read aloud.
- **📖 Psalms** — all **150**, each tagged *"go here when…"*, searchable and filterable by kind.
- **🎵 Songs** — **60** songs of the Bible, from the Song of the Sea to the Hallelujahs of heaven, by what to sing them for.
- **🙏 Prayers** — **532 prayers** across all 66 books: browse by **kind** (17), the **patterns** of what God answers, the **24 great prayers**, and the **outcomes** (*granted · denied · delayed · answered-differently · conditional · still-unfolding*).
- **🔊 Read aloud** (SpeechSynthesis), **★ save** your go-to verses, works **offline**, installs to your home screen.

Every reference is from the KJV and was verified against the real text.

## Editing
The whole treasury lives in `app_data.js` (`window.PSS`). Change wording there, then **bump the `CACHE` version in `sw.js`** so installed devices pick it up.
